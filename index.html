Lottery Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Lottery Vault ("the app") has no accounts and no sign-in. The tickets you save, your results, your spending ledger and your settings are stored only on your phone. The developer has no server that receives this data and cannot see it.

Lottery Vault is an independent record-keeping tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Powerball, Mega Millions, MUSL or any state lottery, and it does not sell tickets or accept wagers.

1. Data stored on your phone

  • Ticket numbers, game, drawing dates, play options (Power Play, Double Play, multiplier), notes, result checks, ledger entries (date, game, amount spent, amount won, memo) and app settings are kept in the app's local storage on your device.
  • For the free plan, the app counts how many tickets you scanned in the current month (a month label and a number) on your device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, SSN or any ID document.

2. Camera and ticket scanning

  • The app asks for camera permission only when you open Scan a ticket. You can refuse and enter tickets by hand.
  • Ticket text is read on your device by Google ML Kit Text Recognition, whose model is bundled inside the app. Photos are not uploaded. Photos taken in the app are deleted when you leave the scan screen; the recognised numbers are saved only after you check them and tap Save.
  • Choose a photo uses the Android system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick. The app does not ask for storage permission.
  • Like all ML Kit features, ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information (for example device and app information, performance metrics, API configuration and error codes) to improve the service. This does not include your photos or the recognised text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

3. Getting drawing results

  • To check your tickets, the app downloads published winning numbers. It only downloads; it never sends your tickets or numbers.
  • Sources: our results file hosted on GitHub Pages (https://jy0714ryu.github.io/lotteryvault-feed/v1/) and, as a fallback you can turn off in Settings, New York State open data (data.ny.gov). As with any website request, these hosts receive your IP address and basic request information under their own policies (GitHub, data.ny.gov).

4. Notifications (optional)

  • Drawing alerts are off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your phone. No push server is used.

5. Backup (optional)

  • Backups are files created on your phone. You choose where to save or share them with the system share sheet. The developer never receives them.
  • Android's own device backup may include app data according to your Google account backup settings. The developer has no access to it.

6. Ads

  • The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic and crash information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Gambling and betting ads are blocked for this app. Lottery Vault Pro removes ads.

7. Purchases

  • Lottery Vault Pro (monthly subscription or lifetime purchase) is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google; the developer does not receive your card or billing address.
  • The app checks with Google Play on your phone whether you own Pro and remembers the answer on your device. There is no developer server.
  • Cancel a subscription in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or from Settings > Manage subscription in the app.

8. Age

  • Lottery Vault is intended for adults 18 and older. On first launch the app asks you to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Where Google Play provides age signals (for example under Texas law), the app asks Google Play for an age range when it starts. The answer is used only to decide whether the app and purchases are available, is kept in memory while the app runs, and is never stored, logged or sent anywhere by the app.
  • The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Sharing and selling

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your tickets or ledger with anyone.
  • The only third parties that receive data from the app are the Google services described above (AdMob, ML Kit diagnostics, Google Play Billing, Google Play age signals) and the result hosts in section 3. In some US states, ad personalisation through AdMob may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out by deleting or limiting your advertising ID in Android settings or by buying Pro.

10. Deleting your data

  • Delete tickets and ledger entries in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage) to delete everything stored by it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing on our side to delete.

11. Changes to this policy

  • If we make a material change, we will update this page and its effective date and announce the change in the app before it takes effect.
